DATA PROVENANCE AND CORRECTIONS
===============================
data/raw/hudaverdi2010_local_90.csv
  Converted from Blast_Database_Combined.csv (which is actually an .xlsx workbook, not CSV).
  90 blasts = Kulatilake et al. (2010) Tables 1 & 2 (training sets only).

data/raw/external/hudaverdi_blast_fragmentation_finaldataset.csv
  Downloaded 2026-09-29 from https://github.com/pranjalkaushik-in/Blast-Design-and-Rock-Properties
  110 blasts = Hudaverdi, Kulatilake & Kuzu (2011) IJNAMG 35:1318-1333 database:
  97 model-development + 13 validation blasts (incl. 7 Miami Mine, Arizona blasts not in Kulatilake 2010).
  Third-party transcription -> MUST be spot-checked against the original IJNAMG tables before publication.
  Validation rows (En13, Ru7, Mg8, Mg9, Rc1, Mr12, Db10, Sm8, Oz8, Oz9, Ad23, Ad24) were checked:
  their X50 matches Kulatilake et al. (2010) Tables 4 & 5 exactly.

data/raw/external/blast_fragmentation_synthetic_dataset.csv
  SYNTHETIC (simulated) data mimicking Gebretsadik et al. (2024). NOT real measurements.
  Do NOT use for training/evaluation claims in the paper.

data/processed/blast_master_110.csv  (the file to upload to Kaggle)
  Cross-check local vs external on 90 overlapping blasts: 2 discrepancies, resolved against Kulatilake (2010):
    Oz7 Pf : external 0.53 -> corrected to 0.70 (Table 2 of Kulatilake 2010)
    Ad22 E : local 15.0 wrong -> 16.9 kept (Table 2 of Kulatilake 2010; all Akdaglar blasts E = 16.9 GPa)
  Columns: blast_id, site, mine, country, rock_type, E_group (G1 E>=40 GPa / G2), paper_split
           (train/validation as in the original paper), S_B, H_B, B_D, T_B, Pf [kg/m3], XB [m], E [GPa], X50 [m], source
